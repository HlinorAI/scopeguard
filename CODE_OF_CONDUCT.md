# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in ScopeGuard welcoming, respectful and constructive for everyone.

Expected behavior includes:

- being respectful and professional;
- giving and receiving actionable feedback;
- focusing on the work and its impact;
- respecting privacy and confidentiality;
- assuming good intent while addressing harmful behavior clearly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information and disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Report concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
